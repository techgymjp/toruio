```
9回、一点を争う場面。代走として一塁に立ったあなたの仕事はただひとつ――盗むこと。
二盗、三盗、そしてホームスチールまで。100回のチャレンジで、何個の盗塁を決められるか？

■ リードと牽制の駆け引き
・リードボタンを押している間カニ歩きでリード、離すと帰塁

・投げるふりの偽投から、すぐに投球してくる投手も

・投手が投げた瞬間に自動でスタート。ダッシュボタンで全力疾走

・ヘッドスライディングは一番速いが、早すぎれば届かず、遅すぎればオーバースライド

・相手の投手はクイックの速さ・牽制の多さ、捕手は肩の強さがチャレンジごとに変わる

■ ナイターの球場と大歓声
・ランナーの背中越しに次の塁を見る3D視点。満員のスタンドと照明塔
・トランペットと太鼓の応援、走り出すと「おおっ」とどよめく
・盗塁を決めるたびに花火と大歓声。ホームスチールなら特大の花火

■ その他
・「草野球の代走」から「伝説の走り屋」までの称号
・二盗・三盗・ホームスチールそれぞれの成功率を結果画面に表示

・オフラインでも遊べる（広告とシェアには通信が必要）
```

**Name** (max 30 characters)


**Subtitle** (max 30 characters)


**Promotional text** (max 170 characters)

```
Pinch run from first and steal second, third — and home! Take a lead, beat the pickoff, go on the pitch and dive in head-first. 100 tries, up to 300 steals.
```

**Description** (max 4000 characters)

```
Bottom of the ninth, one run in it. You're sent in to pinch run at first base, and you have one job: steal.
Second, third, and even home. 100 tries — how many bases can you steal?

■ THE LEAD-AND-PICKOFF DUEL
• Hold LEAD to shuffle off the bag, let go to get back
• A bigger lead means a better jump — and more pickoff throws
• When you see "PICKOFF!", get back. Too far off? Dive back head-first
• Watch out for fake throws followed by a quick pitch

■ YOU GO ON THE PITCH — THE REST IS SPEED AND GUTS
• You take off automatically on the pitch. Hold DASH to sprint
• Slide before the bag — the button flashes "NOW!" when the timing is right
• Head-first is the fastest, but go too early and you come up short, too late and you overslide
• Feet-first is a bit slower but safe. Don't slide at all and you save your stamina
• Bang-bang plays go to slow motion. Hold your breath for the umpire's call

■ MANAGE YOUR STAMINA
• Every lead and every slide costs stamina and slows you down
• Burn it all on second and third, and you'll have nothing left for home
• Each try brings a new battery: how quick the pitcher is to the plate, how often he throws over, and how strong the catcher's arm is

■ NIGHT GAME ATMOSPHERE
• 3D view from behind the runner, looking at the next base, with packed stands and light towers
• Trumpet-and-drum cheering, and the crowd roars when you take off
• Fireworks and cheers for every steal — and a huge show when you steal home

■ FEATURES
• Ranks from Sandlot Runner to Legend of the Base Paths
• Success rates for second, third and home on the results screen
• Got thrown out? Watch an ad to retry that steal (up to 5 times per game)
• Share your results with friends
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese

Play ball! Can you become the Steal King?
```
