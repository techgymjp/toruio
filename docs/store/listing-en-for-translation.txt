[Full description] (max 4000 characters)
You are a runner on first base in a baseball game at night. Your job is to steal bases. Steal second base, then third base, then home plate. You have 100 tries. How many bases can you steal?

■ HOW TO PLAY
• Hold the LEAD button to move away from the base. Release the button to go back to the base.
• A big lead helps you run faster to the next base. But the pitcher throws to the base more often.
• When the pitcher throws to your base, go back quickly. You can dive back to the base.
• Sometimes the pitcher only pretends to throw. Then he pitches quickly.
• When the pitcher pitches, you start running automatically. Hold the DASH button to run fast.
• Slide into the base. The slide button flashes when it is the right time to press it.
• A head-first slide is the fastest. If you slide too early, you stop before the base. If you slide too late, you slide past the base and you are out.
• A feet-first slide is slower, but you do not slide past the base.

■ STAMINA
• Each lead and each slide uses stamina. With less stamina, you run more slowly.
• Your stamina comes back when you start a new try.
• Save your stamina for the steal of home plate.

■ PITCHERS AND CATCHERS
• You see a new pitcher and a new catcher on each try.
• Some pitchers are quick. Some pitchers throw to the base often.
• Some catchers have a strong arm.

■ 3D STADIUM AT NIGHT
• You see the field from behind the runner.
• The crowd plays trumpets and drums.
• Fireworks go up every time you steal a base.
• You see a big fireworks show when you steal home plate.

■ FEATURES
• Get a rank based on how many bases you steal.
• See your success rate for second base, third base and home plate.
• Share your results with your friends.
• Play without an internet connection. Ads and sharing need a connection.
• English and Japanese are available in the game.
